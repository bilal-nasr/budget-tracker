# Cashier Payments and Change Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use `superpowers:executing-plans` to implement this plan task by task after the orchestrator authorizes implementation. Steps use checkboxes. Do not stage, commit, push, create worktrees, or dispatch agents without separate authorization.

**Goal:** Optionally record cash tender and returned change on a purchase, including change in another wallet currency, with correct expenses and balances.

**Architecture:** Keep a single expense transaction as the purchase aggregate. Its existing `amount_minor` remains purchase cost in the paying wallet's currency; additional settlement fields record the actual tender and actual returned change. Update the balance projection to use settlement movements while reports continue using cost. Use one authenticated SQL RPC for a purchase save, integrating item 2's breakdown children in that same database transaction.

**Tech stack:** Next.js 16.4.5 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, zod 4, Supabase Postgres/RLS, next-intl, existing SheetShell/Amount/keypad primitives.

**Spec:** Owner item 4 in the dispatch, with proposed design below. These are recommendations, not owner-approved decisions. Related contracts: item 2 expense breakdowns and item 6 SYP currency support.

## Proposed design/spec

### Cost and settlement policy

1. The main amount remains **Purchase cost**, denominated in the selected paying wallet's currency. Default expense flow is unchanged. Optional **Cash paid and change** disclosure exposes **Paid to cashier**, **Change received**, **Change currency**, and **Put change in**. No cashier contact/entity is needed.
2. Let `P` = purchase cost, `T` = tender, `C` = actual change minor units in the selected receiving wallet. `P,T` are positive integers up to MAX_MINOR; `C` is 0..MAX_MINOR. Require `T >= P`. With zero change require `T=P` and persist no change-wallet/currency/rate fields. Same source/receiving wallet is allowed for same currency; another same-currency wallet is also allowed.
3. Same currency: require `T-P=C` exactly, no rate. Default receiver to paying wallet. Wallet delta is `-T` plus `+C` if it receives change; same-wallet net is `-P`. No income row, transfer row, or second purchase debit.
4. Different currency: require an active owned wallet in the selected change currency and a **confirmed transaction rate**. Persist actual `C`, not a reference-rate estimate. Reconcile using exactly one direction: `P = T - roundHalfUp(convert C from change currency to paying currency at confirmed rate)`. Require the converted change to be positive and less than `T`. Reject discrepancy rather than silently alter cost, tender, or actual change. Show the source-currency value of change as an approximation and explain the rounding unit; the confirmed snapshot is the accounting valuation, not a live market truth.
5. For initial USD/LBP support, rate is explicitly **1 USD = R LBP**, using existing integer rate bounds. Example: `P=1250 USD cents`, `T=2000 USD cents`, `C=671250 LBP`, `R=89500`; persisted expense cost $12.50, USD wallet delta -$20, LBP wallet delta +671,250, income unchanged. Reverse example: LBP cost 895,000, tender 1,790,000, USD change 1,000 cents at 89,500. Same-currency example $12.50 cost, $20 tender, $7.50 change.
6. Reference rate may prefill a suggestion only. User must confirm it for this purchase; store snapshot pair/direction/value, never reread profile rate when displaying, editing, deleting, or restoring history. Do not update the profile from a purchase. If a recorded actual change does not reconcile, ask user to correct the entered cost/rate/change. Do not offer Move's arbitrary received override that would break reconciliation.
7. Item 6 compatibility: item 6 supplies the currency registry and guards the legacy USD/LBP converter; its SYP Move flow accepts two manual actual amounts and **does not supply SYP rate conversion**. Same-currency SYP cashier settlement can use that registry immediately. This cashier increment proposes its own explicit direct-rate extension for USD/SYP and LBP/SYP, with canonical labels `1 USD = R SYP` and `1 LBP = R SYP`, supporting payment/change in either direction. Keep USD/LBP labelled `1 USD = R LBP` with its existing integer bounds. Never infer a rate from unrelated transfers or chain through USD. The direct SYP quote accepts up to six decimals, positive and at most 1,000,000,000 quote major units per base major unit; parse exactly to numerator N (1..10^15) and denominator D in {1,10,...,10^6}, then reduce by gcd. These precision/bound choices are proposals for consolidation, not item 6 behavior. Persist pair, N and D per purchase; never save them into the legacy profile rate. If this explicit extension is deferred, disable both SYP cross-change pairs with an explanation; manual transfer support alone cannot enable them.
8. A currency with no active wallet remains discoverable as disabled with explanation: EN “Create an active {currency} wallet to receive change in {currency}.” AR “أنشئ محفظة نشطة بعملة {currency} لاستلام الباقي بهذه العملة.” Provide Wallets navigation; preserve draft while presenting guidance, and require explicit reselection after wallet refresh. Archived-only does not qualify. Server must recheck even if UI enabled it earlier.
9. Breakdown children (item 2) sum to **at most** `P` in purchase currency, never `T` or converted change. Persist children as item 2 defines; show an explicit unitemized remainder `P-sum(children)` (zero is allowed). The parent retains the category; this increment introduces no item-level category allocations or budget-query changes. One purchase row feeds expense reporting once. Activity row's headline is cost; detail shows cost, tender, actual change, both wallets and confirmed rate. Wallet-filtered view may show a change badge but must not label returned change as income or change its cost headline to tender.
10. Recommendations for scope: ordinary manually entered purchases only initially. New cashier metadata is disallowed on salary/fixed/installment-linked rows (`source is not null` or `installment_id is not null`); preserve those historical rows and existing edit behavior without settlement. Explicitly exclude split tender, multiple change currencies, fees/refunds, debt/credit and purchase-pricing currency distinct from paying wallet currency. Permit negative resulting balances as existing wallets do; insufficient funds is not a new blocking policy.

### Persistence and atomicity

Proposed nullable columns on `transactions`: `tender_minor bigint`, `change_wallet_id uuid references wallets(id)` (restrict deletion), `change_minor bigint`, `change_currency public.currency`, `change_rate_base public.currency`, `change_rate_quote public.currency`, `change_rate_numerator bigint`, `change_rate_denominator bigint`. Four rate fields are either all null or all present. Derive/check the pair against paying/receiving wallet currencies and the canonical pair list above. USD/LBP rate snapshots require denominator=1 and numerator within existing MAX_RATE. Reuse item 2's `transactions.revision integer not null default 1` and its before-update trigger; do not add a `version` column or another revision trigger. Use the supplied transaction UUID itself as the stable create-retry key; do not add a separate request-key column.

Proposed `SettlementRate={base:Currency;quote:Currency;quoteMajorNumerator:number;quoteMajorDenominator:number}` and `convertSettlementMinor(minor:number,from:Currency,to:Currency,rate:SettlementRate):number|null`. With scales S_base/S_quote from item 6, base-to-quote conversion is half-up of `minor*N*S_quote/(D*S_base)`; inverse is half-up of `minor*D*S_base/(N*S_quote)`. Use BigInt intermediates in TypeScript and exact numeric integer arithmetic in SQL, validate all operands and output cap, and return null for unsupported/mismatched pair. No floating point division, generic non-USD fallback or triangular conversion. The settlement equation still converts actual change to paying currency once; no second inverse conversion requirement. This helper is separate from item 6's pair-guarded legacy `convertAtRate` and does not alter manual Move semantics.

- No settlement: all eight nullable columns null; legacy expenses/income/transfers unchanged. Exact tender without change: tender=P, all change fields null. Positive change: tender and change amount/wallet/currency present; same currency has null rate; cross currency has valid supported-pair rate. Only eligible expense rows can have settlement fields. Reject partial shapes/forged currencies at DB level, not just zod.
- Database integrity derives both currency fields from locked owned wallet rows, validates category, bounds, settlement equation and immutable user. Explicitly compare referenced row `user_id` with transaction `user_id` as well as relying on RLS; RLS alone is insufficient for a caller who tries foreign `user_id` or privileged maintenance paths.
- Lock the union of old/new source, transfer destination and change wallet IDs in ascending UUID order using `FOR UPDATE` before reading active/currency state. This serializes archive/currency/delete races; FK protects historical change wallets even after soft deletion. Extend `wallets_currency_locked` to all three roles, including deleted history.
- Inserts, restores and financially changed settlement require active source and receiving wallets. Allow metadata-only edits and soft delete of historical rows with archived wallets; restoring with an archived wallet returns actionable error. A changed wallet ID/currency must reset and revalidate rate/change UI state. Never reinterpret typed money silently on currency switch.
- Balance view source expense contribution becomes `-coalesce(tender_minor,amount_minor)`; credit positive live `change_minor` at `change_wallet_id` independently of transfer credits. Do not also debit purchase cost. Deleted parent contributes nothing. Sums remain SQL integer/numeric; returned JS totals need safe-integer range checks consistent with item 6's aggregate policy.
- Canonical SQL API is item 2's `save_transaction`, with no separate purchase-save function. Its exact incremental adaptation and retry contract are below. It writes cost/settlement and full-list item changes atomically and returns the final `revision` after item touches.
- Reuse `set_transaction_deleted(p_id uuid,p_expected_revision integer,p_deleted boolean) returns table(id uuid,revision integer)` from item 2. Lock the owned parent and compare the exact revision before inspecting/changing state; all children follow parent visibility. An already-achieved state is a no-op only at that exact current revision. Never accept expected+1 or another stale revision as proof of an identical retry; a lost lifecycle response requires reload/reconciliation. Restore revalidates active wallets and linked guards. No zero-row update reports success. Generic update actions cannot convert a settled expense into income while retaining settlement.

### Reconciled item 2 API and precise incremental adaptations

The orchestrator selected this canonical contract shared with item 2:

`save_transaction(p_id uuid,p_expected_revision integer,p_input jsonb,p_items jsonb) returns table(id uuid,revision integer)`.

The nonnull supplied `p_id` is a stable UUID allocated once per new draft. `p_expected_revision=null` means create or identical create replay; nonnull means revision-guarded update of that existing owned live row. `p_input` is the strictly validated complete editable parent input (`kind`, `occurredOn`, `walletId`, `amountMinor`, `categoryId`, optional `note`), excluding items/settlement, trusted currency, revision, source/link fields and ownership. Keep one canonical normalizer for omitted/empty note. Transfers remain outside this save RPC. Preserve source/fixed/installment links, item 2's shared integer `revision`, parent/child locks/deferred checks and saved-item currency lock. Return final revision after all child touches; a save may increment it several times. Items remain `{description,amountMinor}` and parent category remains the sole budget classification.

In item 4's subsequent append-only migration, replace this four-argument function with one five-argument function by appending `p_settlement jsonb default null`. Drop the old exact signature and create the new defaulted signature in the same migration, restoring grants; do not leave ambiguous PostgREST overloads. Existing named four-argument calls resolve through the default. Do not introduce a differently named aggregate save, a second concurrency column, the superseded flat-parameter API, or a separate request UUID.

- `p_items=null`: create with empty items; on update preserve saved items. `[]` explicitly clears; an array replaces the final complete list. Keep item 2's SQL validation, saved-item currency guard and final sum <= P check. Never compute the remainder against tender.
- `p_settlement=null`: create plain expense; update preserves settlement. `{}` explicitly clears settlement; a strict `CashSettlementInput` object replaces it. Revalidate preserved/replaced cost, kind, source/change currency and item sum as one aggregate. An older caller changing cost on a settled row must receive mismatch if the old settlement no longer reconciles. Converting to income requires explicit clearing and must satisfy item rules too.
- Create retry: use `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING`, then select/lock only an owned row. Require it to be live and normalized current parent input, complete positional description/amount list (not recreated item IDs) and settlement snapshot to equal the supplied create. Identical replay returns the existing id/current final revision without touching children, changing revision, or applying movements again. Different/deleted replay, inaccessible/colliding owner, or changed current payload returns conflict; never resurrect or overwrite. Both first and repeated create use the same supplied p_id and null expected revision. Concurrent same-ID submissions serialize at the unique constraint; prove final-state equality checks in a two-session test.
- Duplicate allocates a fresh transaction UUID/date/draft item keys, never source links, and reconfirms cross-rate. New draft gets a fresh UUID; retry in an existing draft retains it. Updates never regenerate that ID and require the originally read revision.
- Selected TypeScript adaptation for the orchestrator to apply consistently to items 2/4: `createTransaction(input:TransactionInput,id:string):Promise<ActionResult<{id:string;revision:number}>>`; `updateTransaction(id:string,input:TransactionInput,expectedRevision:number)` returns the same success payload. Both map to the one `save_transaction` RPC. `TransactionInput.items` follows item 2; `settlement?:CashSettlementInput|null` is expense-only, where omitted maps to SQL null/preserve and explicit null maps to `{}`/clear. Validate ID/revision as well as money and lines before DB access.
- Canonical lifecycle RPC from item 2 is `set_transaction_deleted(p_id uuid,p_expected_revision integer,p_deleted boolean) returns table(id uuid,revision integer)`. Reuse it, adding settlement wallet restoration checks in this increment. `deleteTransaction(id:string,expectedRevision:number)` and `restoreTransaction(id:string,expectedRevision:number)` return the same `{id,revision}` success. No optional-revision bypass is proposed. All detail and undo callers pass the read/result revision; restore uses the revision returned by delete. Add success/error handling to undo rather than discarding failed results.
- Upgrade existing salary/fixed/installment/transfer creation success payloads to include the inserted revision (select `id,revision`, no extra round trip) so their Undo callers can use the canonical delete signature. This is shared revision integration from item 2, not permission to enable cashier metadata on linked expenses. Relevant additional files: `src/features/{salary,fixed,installments}/actions.ts`, `src/components/{salary/salary-card,fixed/fixed-card,installments/installment-pay-sheet}.tsx`, plus transfer creation and toast in transactions/actions and add/transaction-sheet. Item 2 should own these call-site changes once; item 4 preserves them.

- Use invoker-security RPCs, `set search_path=''`, schema-qualified objects, revoke execute from public/anon and grant only authenticated; no arbitrary user ID parameter. DB triggers/checks enforce financial integrity for direct authenticated table writes too. Preserve existing installment trigger ordering/guards when replacing shared functions.

## Review focus

- Wallet archived/deleted or currency changed after the sheet opens: save fails atomically with actionable wallet error, no partial expense/children.
- Lost response/double submit/stale edit: one purchase only, no overwrite of a newer edit, and undo cannot delete a changed purchase unexpectedly.
- Actual cash differs from an estimate: persist entered change exactly or block with reconciliation guidance; never silently alter it.
- Change-only wallet history: currency locked, physical delete blocked, soft delete/undo changes both wallet balances together.
- Cross-currency purchase with partial breakdown: parent cost counted once, no income inflation, SYP cannot enter the USD/LBP fallback conversion.

## Alternatives and consequential decisions for consolidation

- **Recommended one-row settlement vs expense + linked exchange:** one row keeps expense reporting unchanged and delete/restore inherently atomic. Linked exchange would debit P then residual tender and credit change, but requires protected child ownership/lifecycle and hiding generated rows everywhere. Avoid standalone income/change row (inflates income) or expense T plus transfer residual (double debit).
- **Recommended source-currency cost with explicit confirmed rate:** keeps existing category/budget currency truthful and records actual cash. It requires exact reconciliation at one source minor unit. Allowing arbitrary mismatch would need a separately reported fee/rounding adjustment; recommend excluding that until requested.
- Material unresolved: owner may intend entering a shop price in LBP while paying USD. Recommend converting explicitly to source-currency purchase cost for this scope; supporting independent pricing currency needs an additional quoted-price snapshot and reporting decision.
- Material unresolved: whether to enable the proposed cashier-only SYP direct-rate extension (USD/SYP and LBP/SYP, six decimal quote precision) in this increment. Item 6 deliberately provides no such rate feature. Recommendation is the explicit extension above, with disabling these pairs as a coherent staged alternative. Integer LBP/USD may not fit every cashier quote; changing its precision is a separate shared money-policy decision.
- Recommended limitation requiring consolidation: cashier settlement enabled only for manual expenses initially, not fixed/installment payment editors. Existing linked expenses continue to work with unchanged guards. If owner wants cash tender for installments too, preserve principal=P and extend the coordinated RPC with link validation before enabling that UI.
