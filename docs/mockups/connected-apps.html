Settings: Connected apps

A new section right after Sign-in keys, built like it: a titled section, one white list inlay, and the "Connect Claude" help as a second inlay that opens by itself when nothing is connected and stays folded once an app is. Each frame is Settings scrolled to the block. The address and app ID stay left-to-right in Arabic, wrap instead of cutting off, and select whole on a long press. In the empty frames the address shows its "Copied" state for two seconds after a tap. Remove is a quiet text button; the confirm sheet is the shared ConfirmSheet with its danger tone. The app chip is a generic icon, never a logo, as on the consent screen. Synthetic data only.
