# Surface brief: app shell and design system (all screens)

Mode: Operate. Phone first (390px), then tablet/desktop. English + Arabic (RTL), light first, dark follows.
Audience/job: the owner logging money on the go, one-handed; month-end review. Tasks: add, find, check budget, see wallets.
Chosen in the direction round: world A "Encaustic tile" (assigned, seed d85b1cf1). Amount entry: in-sheet keypad on touch. Arabic digits: Latin.
Unresolved: dark theme palette, desktop sidebar density, reports chart styling.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Lebanese encaustic cement tile as a category and progress language. Every category owns a geometric tile motif; budget progress is a row of tile cells that fill. Refuses the grey screen with one accent and the hero-metric card.

OWN-WORLD: Deep-teal shell (#0F4C4A) over a cool limestone ground (#ECEFEA), white surfaces, ink #14211F. Tile palette teal, oxblood (#8E2F3A), ochre (#C98F10). Marigold (#F2B632) appears only on the Add button: the one thing you can do next. Oxblood-red (#A3302A) only for over-budget, always with an icon and text. Readex Pro (Latin and Arabic share one design), tabular figures for amounts. Small 6px-radius tile glyph chips, 12px cards.

STORY: Open the app, see in one glance what is left to spend in each currency, notice anything over budget, and tap the marigold Add button to log in four taps.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home at 390x844. Teal block under the status bar: month switcher and settings, then the sentence "Left to spend this month", the USD amount at 40px with a 16-cell tile bar and spent/of line, the LBP amount at 28px with its own tile bar. Below: an over-budget alert (icon, text, See budget). Then Recent list and the wallets strip. Bottom bar, five columns, raised marigold Add in the centre; teal bar with white active label.

FORM: Assigned candidate 3 of 7 (encaustic cement tile), seed d85b1cf1. Raises: monumental single answer first (airport signage); alert colour reserved for change, only over-budget (boarding pass); one actionable hue only (orienteering).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
